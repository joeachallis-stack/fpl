# FPL 2026/27 Season Rules

Source: official Premier League FPL site, checked 2026-09-02. Re-verify each season —
rules change year to year.

## Chips
- Two full sets across the season: **Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain, Bench Boost** —
  one of each per half. Eight chips total.
- Only one chip may be played per gameweek.
- First-half chips must be used by the **Gameweek 19 deadline: 13:30 GMT, Saturday 2 January
  2027**. They do not carry over into the second half.
- Second-half chips refresh at that point and run to the end of the season.
- Assistant Manager chip status is unclear from public reporting as of this writing — treat
  as absent unless confirmed in-game.

## Transfers
- Up to **5 free transfers can be banked/rolled** at once.
- No extra bonus transfers this season (no AFCON mid-season disruption).
- Wildcard = unlimited free transfers for that gameweek, permanent squad change.
- Free Hit = unlimited free transfers for one gameweek only; squad reverts at the next
  deadline.

## Scoring
- Defensive contribution points retained (points for defenders/midfielders hitting
  tackle+interception+clearance thresholds — check in-game rules for exact thresholds,
  they can shift).
- Bonus Points System (BPS) tweaked to reduce overlap with defensive contribution points
  and to improve bonus-point odds for goalkeepers, full-backs, and attacking players.
- Live/projected bonus points now appear ~20 minutes into a match and update as play
  continues.

## Deadlines
- Gameweek "lockdown" (when official post-match data, e.g. bonus points, is finalized)
  now happens at **09:00 UK time the day after** the final match of the gameweek, not one
  hour after the last final whistle. Live bonus points may still shift until then.
- Standard transfer deadline: 90 minutes before the first kickoff of the gameweek (as in
  prior seasons) — always double-check the exact time via `bootstrap.json` →
  `events[].deadline_time`.

## What to re-check each season
- Chip count/structure
- Free transfer cap
- Defensive contribution thresholds and point values
- Any new chip (e.g. Assistant Manager) — confirm directly in-app since press coverage
  has been inconsistent
