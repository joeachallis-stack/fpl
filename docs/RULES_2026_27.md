# FPL 2026/27 Season Rules

Source: official Premier League FPL site, checked 2026-09-02. Re-verify each season —
rules change year to year.

## Chips
- Two full sets across the season: **Wildcard, Free Hit, Triple Captain, Bench Boost** —
  one of each per half. Eight chips total.
- Only one chip may be played per gameweek.
- First-half chips must be used by the **Gameweek 19 deadline: 13:30 GMT, Saturday 2 January
  2027**. They do not carry over into the second half.
- Second-half chips refresh at that point and run to the end of the season.
- **No Assistant Manager chip.** Confirmed 2026-09-03 from two directions: every `mng_*`
  key in `game_config.scoring` is 0, and the chip ran only in 2024/25 — vaastav's data has
  322 `AM` position rows that season (GW23–38) and none in 2025/26, when it was replaced by
  the two-set chip system still in force now.

## Transfers
- Up to **5 free transfers can be banked/rolled** at once (confirmed via
  `game_settings.max_extra_free_transfers` = 4).
- No extra bonus transfers this season, and **no AFCON mid-season disruption** —
  confirmed 2026-09-02: AFCON 2027 runs 19 June – 17 July 2027, a summer tournament
  entirely outside this season. (A GW16 free-transfer top-up claim in
  [`PRIOR_ART.md`](PRIOR_ART.md) is a carryover from 2025/26, when AFCON 2025 ran
  Dec 2025 – Jan 2026. It does not apply this season; `scripts/state.py` needs no
  GW16 special case.)
- Wildcard = unlimited free transfers for that gameweek, permanent squad change.
- Free Hit = unlimited free transfers for one gameweek only; squad reverts at the next
  deadline.

## Scoring
- **Defensive contribution: +2 points, at these thresholds — DEF 10, MID 12, FWD 12.**
  Composition differs by position: **DEF counts tackles + CBI only**; **MID and FWD also
  count ball recoveries**; **GKP never scores it** (always 0, even with double-digit
  recoveries). Capped at +2 per match.

  *Derived and verified, not copied.* The threshold appears nowhere in the API. It was
  obtained by reconstructing the full scoring formula and running it over all 29,757
  player-gameweeks of 2025/26 (vaastav `data/2025-26/gws/merged_gw.csv`): the highest-
  scoring non-qualifying defender sits on 9 and the lowest qualifying on 10, with the
  same clean boundary at 11/12 for MID and FWD. Independently agrees with the published
  Premier League rules and with [`PRIOR_ART.md`](PRIOR_ART.md). The *composition* is
  documented nowhere and came from the data alone. Safe to hardcode.
- Bonus Points System (BPS) tweaked to reduce overlap with defensive contribution points
  and to improve bonus-point odds for goalkeepers, full-backs, and attacking players.
- Live/projected bonus points now appear ~20 minutes into a match and update as play
  continues.

## Deadlines
- Gameweek "lockdown" (when official post-match data, e.g. bonus points, is finalized)
  now happens at **09:00 UK time the day after** the final match of the gameweek, not one
  hour after the last final whistle. Live bonus points may still shift until then.
- Standard transfer deadline: 90 minutes before the first kickoff of the gameweek (as in
  prior seasons) — always double-check the exact time via `bootstrap.json` →
  `events[].deadline_time`.
