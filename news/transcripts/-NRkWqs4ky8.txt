The FPL Gameweek 2 deadline is on Friday, so here is some final advice. Don't overreact to Gameweek 1. One match is an incredibly small sample size, so don't go buying players just because they hauled or selling good players just because they blanked. Try to remain patient. On that note, if you can, it is best to try and roll the transfer in Gameweek 2 to give you another week of information to collect and allow you to make a double move potentially in Gameweek 3. If you do want to make a transfer, the teams with the best fixtures from Gameweek 2 to Gameweek 5 are Newcastle, Spurs, Leeds, and Liverpool. So, I'd be targeting players from those four teams. With the pair blanking in Gameweek 1, I know a lot of people will look away from Bruno Fernandes and Erling Haaland for captaincy, but I still think they're the best two captains for Gameweek 1. And I think at the moment, I'm leaning towards captaining Bruno Fernandes. Whilst the wildcard may be tempting this week, my favorite two weeks to wildcard are actually Gameweek 4 where you can jump on triple Chelsea and the transfer window is shut, or Gameweek 6 which is during the three-week international break at the end of September. Honestly, Gameweek 2 is much more about what you don't do with your team. Remain patient and trust the team that you started the season with.
